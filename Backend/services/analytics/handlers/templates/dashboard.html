1. Executive Overview

This section gives the fastest summary of what is happening in the product right now: activity volume, reliability, data quality, and overall operational health.

Start here first. These charts and KPI cards answer the question: is the product active, stable, and healthy enough to trust the deeper analysis below?

Most Viewed Screens

Shows which screens attract the most attention and where user traffic concentrates.

Most Frequent Events

Highlights the product actions users trigger most often across the application.

App Version Distribution

Shows which versions of the client are currently active and how usage is distributed between releases.

Operational Snapshot

A compact summary of processing lag, backlog, failures, and freshness of the current analytics snapshot.

2. Engagement & Retention

This section explains how deeply users engage with the product and whether they come back over time.

Here we move from general product health to user behavior. These metrics show intensity of usage, habit formation, and retention quality.

Retention by Cohort

Tracks return behavior for different user cohorts across D1, D7, D14, and D30 windows.

Feature Usage by Product Area

Shows which product domains are most actively used and where user attention accumulates.

3. Funnels & User Journeys

This section shows how users move through important scenarios and where drop-off appears in multi-step flows.

Funnels help answer a very practical question: where do users successfully progress and where do they lose momentum?

Workout Sharing Funnel

Measures how many users move from opening the sharing flow to successfully finishing it.

Registration to First Workout

Shows the path from creating an account to reaching the first meaningful training milestone.

Feed Entry to Interaction

Shows whether opening the feed leads to real interaction with posts and social content.

Profile Entry to Relationship Action

Measures whether profile views convert into follows, messages, or deeper social actions.

4. Research & Product Hypotheses

This section acts as a small product laboratory and compares meaningful user groups against each other.

Instead of only counting usage, these comparisons help test product assumptions: whether social activity, sharing, and workout completion are associated with stronger engagement.

Social Activity vs Non-Social Activity

Compares engagement depth between users who perform social actions and those who do not.

Sharing vs Non-Sharing Users

Shows whether workout sharing is associated with broader and more frequent product usage.

Workout Completion vs Engagement

Explores whether completing workouts is linked to deeper overall engagement with the app.

Feature Groups vs Retention Proxy

Shows which product areas appear most associated with active usage and retention-related behavior.

5. Reliability, Data Quality & Pipeline

This section focuses on trust: whether the system is stable, whether analytics data is clean, and whether the ingestion pipeline behaves as expected.

These charts help verify not only the product, but also the analytics subsystem itself. This is the place to inspect errors, invalid events, quality drift, and processing issues.

Error Hot Spots by Screen

Shows where errors concentrate most strongly and which product surfaces deserve investigation first.

Data Quality by App Version

Highlights versions that produce more invalid payloads, unknown events, or broken contracts.

Recent Invalid Events

Shows the newest rejected events with reasons, useful for debugging client contract drift.

Errors vs Drop-Off Proxy

Compares behavior of sessions with errors and sessions without errors to estimate potential drop-off pressure.
